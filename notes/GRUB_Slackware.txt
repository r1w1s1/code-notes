Using Grub 2.12 in Slackware-current (15.1)

        grub-install --target=x86_64-efi --efi-directory=/boot/efi/ --bootloader-id=Slackware --recheck
        grub-mkconfig -o /boot/grub/grub.cfg

Everytime update the kernel you should run:

        grub-mkconfig -o /boot/grub/grub.cfg

OR (this script comes with grub package)

        update-grub
